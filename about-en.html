About Us

Your game starts here.

AYO is the sports platform where players, communities and venues meet.

We exist to get more people playing sport, more often. With AYO, players find people to play with, book courts, join competitions and keep score live, while venues run their business with AYO Venue Management.

The AYO story

AYO started in 2016 as a community helping amateur teams find other teams to play against.

Today, it’s where people find partners for padel, badminton, tennis and more, book courts, join competitions and keep score live. Our goal hasn’t changed since day one: make sport easier to start and more fun to keep playing.

Milestone 01 / 05

A community for finding sparring partners

Mission

Get more people playing sport, more often — because more people playing builds healthier communities and, in time, stronger athletes.

Vision

A sustainable sports ecosystem that raises the social and economic value of grassroots sport through technology.

The AYO ecosystem

Two apps, one goal for the community.

Players and venues use different apps, but every booking lands in the same place — so finding a game and filling a court happen together.
