Why Open Match

Why play Open Match on AYO

Find opponents at your level

Join matches with a skill range that suits you, using PadelLevels or self-rated levels to help find more balanced matchups.

Play to compete—or just for fun

Play competitively and put your rating on the line, or choose Casual and enjoy the game without affecting your skill rating.

Results everyone can agree on

The host submits the score, and players confirm it—so everyone can trust the recorded match result.

Your court, already sorted

Link your match to your AYO court booking, keeping your venue and playing time clear without coordinating a separate reservation.

Set up a match in minutes.

From court booking to confirmed results, manage every step in one place.

01

Set up your match

Choose your court, match format, and playing mode. Create an individual or community match, with or without an existing AYO booking.

02

Invite players or open spots

Invite players directly, share your match for others to join, or approve join requests based on skill level.

03

Set your price and payment method

Choose to collect payments online, in cash, through both methods, or make the match free.

04

Connect Your Match to a Court Booking

Display the “Booked via AYO” label to earn participants’ trust and reassure them that the court is reserved.

05

Record scores and share results

Enter scores during or after the match. Once players confirm the results, share the final standings and winner banner.

Find a match. Play with confidence.

Find a game at your level, secure your spot, and confirm the result, all in one flow.

01

Find your match

Browse matches near you and filter by sport, location, time, playing mode, and skill level.

02

Choose your spot and join

Pick an available spot and pay online or at the venue. No host approval needed.

03

Confirm the Score

Follow the score, then confirm the final result or suggest a correction. If you don’t respond within 24 hours, the score is automatically confirmed.

04

Rate your Host

Review your host’s punctuality, sportsmanship, and communication.

05

Build your Level

Competitive padel match results also count toward your PadelLevels rating.
