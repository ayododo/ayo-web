Why Scoring

Why hosts run social games on Scoring

Run a great game

Choose your players, courts, and format. Scoring generates the rounds and pairings for you—no manual scheduling or complicated calculations.

Play your way

Mix partners, keep teams fixed, or play singles. Choose the format that fits your group and the kind of game you want to run.

Every match counts

Enter scores after each match and watch the leaderboard update live, so everyone can follow the competition as it unfolds.

Make the game worth remembering

Share the final standings and winner banners on WhatsApp or Instagram, or download the results as a PDF.
